# 0711 Process Update — MODIS vs VIIRS agreement (confusion matrices)

Comparison of the MODIS pipeline against the VIIRS `VNP14A1` sensitivity layer for
**Zimbabwe, GADM 3.6 ADM2, 2016 exposure window** — 60 districts × 12 months = **720
district-months**. Metrics joined on `adm2_gid` + `month_start`. Generated by
`scripts/modis_viirs_confusion.py`.

## Methodology (what "correlation" and the confusion matrix mean)

- **Correlation** reported in prior updates is the **Pearson** coefficient (linear
  association) across the 720 district-months. We also report **Spearman** (rank
  correlation), which measures agreement on ordering and is insensitive to magnitude scale.
- **Confusion matrix**: each metric is binarized to *fire present* (value > 0) vs *absent*
  per district-month, then cross-tabulated MODIS × VIIRS. **Agreement** = (both + neither) /
  total. **Cohen's κ** = chance-corrected agreement (0 = chance, 1 = perfect; 0.6–0.8 is
  conventionally "substantial").

## 1. Active-fire pixel-days — MODIS `FIRMS` vs VIIRS

|            | VIIRS + | VIIRS − |
|------------|--------:|--------:|
| **MODIS +**|     337 |      45 |
| **MODIS −**|      49 |     289 |

Agreement **86.9%**, κ **0.738**; Pearson **0.984**, Spearman **0.898**.
*Observation:* the two sensors agree on fire presence in ~87% of district-months, and the ~13% of disagreements are split almost evenly between them (45 MODIS-only vs 49 VIIRS-only).

## 2. Max FRP (MW) — MODIS `MOD14A1/MYD14A1` vs VIIRS

|            | VIIRS + | VIIRS − |
|------------|--------:|--------:|
| **MODIS +**|     333 |      49 |
| **MODIS −**|      48 |     290 |

Agreement **86.5%**, κ **0.730**; Pearson **0.729**, Spearman **0.863**.
*Observation:* the modest Pearson (0.73) reflects magnitude scatter — VIIRS reads lower — not disagreement about the pattern, since presence agreement (87%) and rank agreement (Spearman 0.86) are both high.

## 3. Summed FRP (MW) — MODIS vs VIIRS

|            | VIIRS + | VIIRS − |
|------------|--------:|--------:|
| **MODIS +**|     342 |      50 |
| **MODIS −**|      44 |     284 |

Agreement **86.9%**, κ **0.737**; Pearson **0.952**, Spearman **0.893**.
*Observation:* presence and correlation are both high, but VIIRS totals run far below MODIS (≈4,800 MW lower where both detect fire) because VIIRS is a single Suomi-NPP overpass vs MODIS Terra+Aqua — so summed FRP is a pattern check, not a magnitude match.

## Takeaway

Across all three metrics, VIIRS **strongly corroborates MODIS on where and when fire occurs**
(~87% presence agreement, κ ≈ 0.73–0.74) **and on relative intensity ranking**, but **is not a
magnitude-equivalent** — VIIRS reads systematically lower, most so for summed FRP. Use it as an
independent-sensor sensitivity check, not a substitute. (VIIRS coverage begins 2012-01-19, so
pre-2012 waves cannot be cross-checked.)
